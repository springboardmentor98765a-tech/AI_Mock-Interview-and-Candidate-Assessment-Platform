Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

AI Interview Pro is an educational candidate-assessment project. This policy explains what information the platform processes and how it is used.

Information we process

Depending on the features you use, the platform may process your name, email address, account role, authentication information, resume and extracted skills, interview answers, scores, AI-generated feedback, notification preferences, sharing choices, session events, and audio or video interview recordings.

How information is used

  • To authenticate accounts and provide candidate, recruiter, and administrator dashboards.
  • To conduct mock interviews, calculate assessment metrics, and generate feedback.
  • To deliver password-reset codes, interview reminders, and account notifications when enabled.
  • To let candidates share selected interview evidence with chosen recruiters.
  • To maintain platform security, reliability, and activity records.

Google account and Gmail data

Google Sign-In is used only when you choose that login method. A separately authorized Gmail account is used by the platform owner to send transactional emails through the Gmail API. The platform requests only the permission required to send those messages; it does not read or delete mailbox contents.

AI processing and service providers

Interview content may be processed by Google Gemini to generate questions or feedback. The deployed platform also relies on Vercel for the frontend, Render for the backend, and Supabase for database hosting. These providers process information only as needed to operate the platform.

Candidate sharing controls

Candidate reports are not made available to recruiters by default. A candidate can authorize selected recruiters and can later revoke that access. Administrators may access system information required to operate and monitor the platform.

Storage and retention

Account and assessment records are retained while they are needed to provide the service or demonstrate project functionality. Session recordings may be stored temporarily and may be removed when hosting instances restart or storage is cleared. Browser-saved login details remain on the device where the user enabled that feature and can be removed through the saved-login manager.

Security

The platform uses access controls, hashed local-account passwords, authenticated API requests, and candidate consent checks. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so users should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive personal information.

Your choices

You can disable optional email notifications, revoke recruiter access, remove browser-saved logins, and request correction or deletion of your account information by contacting the project owner.

Children

The platform is intended for students, job candidates, recruiters, and project evaluators. It is not designed for children under 13.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when platform functionality or service providers change. The revision date at the top identifies the latest version.
